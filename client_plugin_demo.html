🏛️ SBI CMS Embeddable AI Assistant Plugin

This page demonstrates the Central Gateway Floating Plugin.

Look at the floating widget icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. Click it to ask questions about bank alerts, camera statuses, response times, or infrastructure metrics!

To embed this chatbot in any intranet portal or website on another system, add this script tag: 

<script 
  src="http://<SERVER_IP>:8001/plugin/widget.js" 
  data-sbi-cms-gateway="http://<SERVER_IP>:8001" 
  data-theme="dark" 
  data-position="bottom-right">
</script>
